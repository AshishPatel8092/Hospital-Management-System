💳 How Billing Works

Accepted Payment Method

UPI/QR only — scan the code or use our UPI ID with any app (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm, etc.). We don't accept cards or net banking on the platform.

Payment Verification

After paying, enter the 12-digit transaction ID (UTR) from your UPI app. Your bill is marked paid only once that's verified.

Receipts

Once a bill is marked "Paid" on your dashboard, that's your receipt of record — it shows the amount, method, and transaction ID.

Corporate / TPA Billing

Have insurance or a corporate policy? Visit our Insurance page to check cashless coverage before your visit.

❓ Billing FAQ

I paid but my bill still shows Pending

Double-check the transaction ID (UTR) you entered matches exactly what your UPI app shows for that payment — it must be the full 12-digit number. If it's still stuck, contact us with your UTR and we'll verify it manually.

Can I pay in cash at the hospital?

Appointments booked online are paid via UPI at the time of booking. Walk-in or front-desk billing may offer other methods in person — this only covers online payments made through the website.

How do I get a refund?

For a cancelled appointment or billing error, reach out via Contact Us with your bill details and UTR — refunds are processed to the original payment method within 5-7 working days.

Does insurance cover my bill directly?

For cashless network hospitals, your insurer settles directly and you may not see a bill for the covered portion. For reimbursement claims, you pay first and claim later — see the Insurance page for details.
