✅ Cashless Treatment — How It Works

  1. Check that your insurer/TPA is in our network (see list above, or ask us).
  2. Before a planned admission, share your policy details with our insurance desk to start pre-authorization.
  3. Present your insurance card and photo ID at the billing desk on arrival.
  4. Our team coordinates directly with your insurer for approval — most pre-authorizations are confirmed within a few hours for standard cases.
  5. On discharge, the approved amount is settled directly with your insurer. You only pay for anything outside your policy's coverage.

💰 Reimbursement Claims (Non-Network Insurers)

If your insurer isn't on our cashless network, you pay for treatment upfront (via UPI, see our Billing page) and then claim reimbursement directly from your insurer. Keep these documents ready:

Original bills & receipts Discharge summary Doctor's prescription Diagnostic reports Filled claim form Policy copy & ID proof

❓ Insurance FAQ

Is OPD (outpatient) consultation covered by insurance?

Most standard health policies in India cover hospitalization, not routine OPD visits, unless you specifically have an OPD add-on or a corporate policy that includes it. Check your policy wording, or ask us using the form below.

How long does cashless pre-authorization take?

For planned admissions, 2-6 hours is typical once we have your complete policy details. Emergency admissions can be treated first, with authorization processed in parallel.

What if my claim gets partially rejected?

This happens for reasons like a policy sub-limit or a non-covered item. You're responsible for the shortfall, but our billing desk can help you understand the breakup and, in some cases, appeal with the insurer.

Can I use insurance for a video/online consultation?

Currently, online appointments booked through this website are paid directly via UPI (see Billing) — insurance is applied for in-person, network-hospital treatment.
