// HEART ATTACK //

Heart Attack Emergency

Symptoms include chest pain, breathing difficulty, sweating and pain in the left arm.

  • Call Ambulance Immediately
  • Keep Patient Calm
  • Loosen Tight Clothing
  • Give Aspirin If Recommended

// STROKE //

Stroke Emergency

Recognize FAST: Face drooping, Arm weakness, Speech difficulty, Time to call help.

  • Call Emergency Services
  • Note Symptom Start Time
  • Keep Patient Comfortable
  • Do Not Give Food

// BURNS //

Burn Injury

Immediate care reduces damage and complications.

  • Cool Burn With Water
  • Do Not Apply Ice
  • Cover With Clean Cloth
  • Seek Medical Help
▶ Watch Video
// BLEEDING //

Severe Bleeding

Heavy bleeding can become life threatening quickly.

  • Apply Firm Pressure
  • Elevate Wound If Possible
  • Use Clean Bandage
  • Call Ambulance
